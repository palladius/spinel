## 🎯 Executive Summary

When searching for project domain names (such as `spinel.md` at **CHF 39.49** / ~$45 USD), it is important to distinguish between **keyword premium pricing** and **TLD baseline registry pricing**:

1. **The `.md` Floor Price:**  
   The **CHF 39.49** price tag is not a surcharge on the word `spinel`. It is the standard **baseline annual registry fee** set by Moldova's ccTLD registry (`nic.md`) and passed down through registrars (Infomaniak, Porkbun, INWX). **Every standard `.md` name will cost ~CHF 39–48/year.**
2. **Alternative TLDs can cut costs by 70%–85%:**  
   Choosing a modern developer TLD (such as `.dev` at ~$12/year or `.app` at ~$14/year) reduces long-term holding costs significantly while providing automatic HSTS security.
3. **Top Ruby-Like Gemstone Candidates:**  
   If staying on `.md` or pivoting to `.dev` / `.app`, the strongest thematic fits are:
   - 🔴 **`pyrope`** (Greek *pyrōpos* = "fire-eyed", blood-red garnet — punchy & unique)
   - 🔴 **`spinel`** (The historical ruby imposter — clean, 6 letters)
   - 🔴 **`corundum`** (The parent mineral of Ruby & Sapphire — authoritative & robust)
   - 🔴 **`rubellite`** (Vibrant red tourmaline — elegant)
   - 🔴 **`carnelian`** (Fiery red-orange quartz — historic stone of scribes & writing)
   - 🔴 **`pezzottaite`** (Raspberry beryl named after Italian mineralogist Federico Pezzotta 🇮🇹)
   - 🔴 **`taaffeite`** (Famous ultra-rare gem famously discovered mistaken for Spinel!)

## 🎨 Branding & Naming Analysis for an Obsidian-Clone

### 1. `spinel.md` / `spinel.app` (The "True Imposter" Angle)
* **Backstory:** Spinel was historically mistaken for ruby in the British Imperial State Crown (the *Black Prince's Ruby*). 
* **Metaphor:** Just as Spinel is the brilliant, lighter alternative to Ruby, this app is the open, git-friendly, cloud-synced alternative to Obsidian.
* **Sound:** 2 syllables, clean, memorable.

### 2. `pyrope.dev` / `pyrope.md` (The "Fiery & Fast" Angle)
* **Backstory:** From ancient Greek *pyrōpos* ("fire-eyed"), a deep blood-red crystal known for clarity and high refractive index.
* **Metaphor:** High-speed Markdown rendering, instant dual-view switching, lightweight local storage.
* **Sound:** Punchy tech aesthetic (like *PyTorch*, *Python*, *Pyro*).

### 3. `corundum.app` / `corundum.dev` (The "Solid Core" Angle)
* **Backstory:** The hardest natural mineral after diamond (Mohs 9); the foundational crystal parent of both Rubies and Sapphires.
* **Metaphor:** Rock-solid zero-knowledge client-side encryption and uncorruptible local Git filesystem storage.
* **Sound:** Industrial, secure, robust.

### 4. `taaffeite.dev` (The "Discovery" Angle)
* **Backstory:** The only gemstone originally identified from a cut and faceted specimen, which had been previously bought as a Spinel!
* **Metaphor:** Uncovering the hidden beauty in structured markdown notes.

### 5. `raspberyl.md` / `raspberyl.app` (The "Raspberry Pi + Beryl" Angle 🍓🇮🇹)
* **Backstory:** *Raspberyl* is the popular trade name for **Pezzottaite** (raspberry beryl), discovered in Madagascar and named after Milanese mineralogist Federico Pezzotta.
* **Metaphor:** Evokes the DIY hacker / Raspberry Pi spirit combined with precious crystal precision.
* **CLI Power Move:** The project domain can be **`raspberyl.md`**, while the snappy CLI tool is named **`pezzotta`** (e.g. `pezzotta init`, `pezzotta sync`, `pezzotta search`) or `raspberyl`!
* **Domain Status:** **100% AVAILABLE** (`raspberyl.md` via `nic.md` / Infomaniak at CHF 39.49).

### 6. `carnelian.dev` / `carnelian.md` (The "Scribe's Stone" Angle)
* **Backstory:** Historically worn by Roman scribes and used for signet rings and wax seals because hot wax did not stick to it.
* **Metaphor:** The pure writer's note-taking tool.
